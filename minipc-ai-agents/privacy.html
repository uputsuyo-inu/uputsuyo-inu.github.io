Privacy Policy

MiniPC AI Agents is used only by its owner, to let AI agents on the owner's own computer work with the owner's own data in the owner's Google account.

  • No data is collected, stored, or shared by the developer. Data goes between the owner's computer and the owner's Google account.
  • To do their work, the AI agents may send this data to the AI model provider that the owner has chosen to use (for example, Anthropic). This happens only at the owner's direction.
  • Authorization tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
